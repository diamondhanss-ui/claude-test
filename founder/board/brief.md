# FareFinder (working name)

- What it is: A simple wrapper around an LLM that takes a traveler's criteria in plain language and finds the cheapest air tickets over the next few months that fit them.
- Who it is for: Flexible leisure travelers who want the lowest fare and can move their dates.
- What it sells, at what price: A free, personal tool (no revenue).
- Where and how: A chat bot (Slack/Telegram/WhatsApp); the user describes criteria in chat and gets ranked cheapest options.
- Budget and constraints: Not stated. Free tool, so no revenue; flight data source not yet chosen.

## Extra context
- The idea started as "create a simple wrapper around LLM for finding cheapest airtickets for the next few months that fit the user criteria".
- No flight-data provider, budget or market size has been chosen or measured.
